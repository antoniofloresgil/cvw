# AI unit: Q31 mode, requantization as muRISCV-NN does it (2026-11-13)

Status: **simulation (`chipsim-ai`, before and after merging upstream's 17 commits into `ai-baseline`): all pass.** Board, area and cycle time: see the end
(filled in as they are measured). Step 3 of [ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-edge-ai-accelerator.md): the unit takes the library's requantization
off the core, which the [record of the wrapper](2026-11-13-wally-fc-s8.md) showed to be most of a layer (6 072 of 10 865 cycles).

## What it does

CTRL bit 3 (Q31, not together with RAW) makes the unit compute, per output,
`y = clamp(muriscv_nn_requantize(W x + bias, MULT, SHIFT) + OFFSET, ACT_MIN, ACT_MAX)` bit for bit as the library's portable scalar code does it
(`muriscv_nn_doubling_high_mult_no_sat`, then `muriscv_nn_divide_by_power_of_two`): MULT is a 32-bit Q31 multiplier, SHIFT signed -31..30 (positive:
the accumulator is shifted left first, as in the library), new registers OFFSET (0x2C), ACT_MIN (0x30), ACT_MAX (0x34), all int32, the low byte of the
clamped value is stored. A start with Q31 and RAW, or a SHIFT outside -31..30, is refused (ERROR), like the other bad arguments.

How it is built, in `src/uncore/ai/ai_unit.sv`:

- The 32 x 32 product is made in **two passes of the multiplier the byte mode already has**, now 32 x 17 signed: the low half of MULT as an unsigned
  16-bit number (stage Q1), the high half signed (stage Q2); the two products are added with the rounding constant 2^30 and taken from bit 31 (stage Q3).
- Stage Q4 is the rounding divide by a power of two (remainder, threshold, increment as in the library), stage Q5 adds OFFSET, clamps and stores the
  byte in its lane of the word. The left shift of the accumulator is done when the pipeline is loaded.
- The pipeline runs **in the background**: it starts when a row's sum is ready and the unit goes on reading the next row; only the write of a word
  (every fourth output, or the last) waits for the pipeline to empty, five clocks. The unit waits for it to be free before starting another row, which
  matters only for rows so short that they take less than five clocks.
- New state: five registers (32 + 49 + 49 + 32 + 32 bits of pipeline, 3 x 32 of registers), one 64-bit adder, a rounding shifter and two comparators.

## What was checked

- `ai/mnn/unit.c` (`ai_wally_fc` in `chipsim-ai`): **140 layers, the unit's bytes equal the library's** (`muriscv_nn_fully_connected_s8`), 127 of them with
  the unit requantizing (the others are a batch of 2 with an output depth that is not a multiple of 4, which the unit's word writes cannot take).
  The first 60 are shapes from 4 x 1 to 1024 x 4, batches 1 and 2, random input and output offsets, multipliers, shifts -16..2, clamps, with and
  without bias, six extreme ones (weights -128, inputs and offsets at the ends); the last 80 use the **whole range** of the requantization: biases of
  every magnitude and sign over int32, multipliers over Q31 (INT32_MIN excluded, the library's documented range), shifts -31..30, offsets and clamps over int32.
- `ai/gemv` refuses Q31 with RAW, shift 31, shift -32 and Y in the trace buffer.
- Lint (`asic_soc_rv32imac_ai`, rv64gc, rv32gc, ...): clean.

## Cycles, 64 x 64 layer, simulation

| | cycles |
|---|---|
| the library's `muriscv_nn_fully_connected_s8` | 84 388 |
| `wally_fc_s8`: the unit in RAW mode plus software requantization | 10 646 |
| **the unit in Q31** (`wally_fc_s8_q31`, as the core sees it) | **1 855** |
| of which the unit's own counter | 1 583 |
| for comparison, byte mode / RAW mode (unit's counter, `ai/gemv`) | 1 504 / 1 650 |

The requantization inside the unit costs about 80 cycles for the layer (1 583 against 1 504), as estimated (2 to 3 per output, hidden behind the next
row's reads except for the drain before a word). The layer is 45 times faster than the library and 5.7 times faster than RAW plus software. The 272 cycles between the
core's 1 855 and the unit's 1 583 (the register writes, the polling, the core stalled while the unit owns the bus) were not separated; in byte mode the
difference was 65.
