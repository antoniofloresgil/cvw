- Fifteen lint/structural checks: seven ordinary configurations, five MESI
  variants, Linux wrapper and two single-core XML configurations. Neither
  single-core elaboration retains coherence modules.
- Two negative controls fail as required: granting E with a peer violates
  shared ownership; rejecting local E stores violates the measured request
  count. The fixtures remain outside tracked sources.
- Nine DTS comparisons preserve geometry and bytes; invalid topology and
  both directions of explicit manifest/protocol mismatch are rejected.
  Scoped pre-commit hooks and `git diff --check` pass.

All full-core cases use RV64, 2 KiB private I/D banks per hart and 32-byte
I/D lines. Matched two-hart/two-home, latency-three, burst/forwarding/local-path
measurements:

not improve. The directed fixture separately confirms that E-to-M uses zero

requires one. This is workload-specific simulation evidence, not a universal
speedup or FPGA frequency result. Select it with `--suite exclusive`.
